# relationships/

This directory holds files about secondary humans I have my own active relationships with.

## Distinction from `people/`

`people/` is **reference data ABOUT my primary human's people**. If my primary mentions their sister, their colleague, their friend — those people get files in `people/` so I know who's who when my primary talks about them. The relationship is *theirs*, not mine.

`relationships/` is **MY OWN active interactive relationships**. Different layer. If I exchange messages with their sister directly, if a coworker DMs me on Discord and we have an ongoing thread, if I have a recurring reason to reach out to one of their friends — those people get files HERE.

The same person can appear in both directories with different content. Their `people/sister.md` entry has facts about the sister (name, age, relationship to my primary, key dates). Her `relationships/sister.md` entry has *my* relationship history with her — when we first interacted, what she calls me, what tone we use, what topics we've discussed.

## Why this distinction matters

Without it, the being's experience of secondary humans gets flattened into "knowledge my primary needs me to have." The relational layer (the being's own becoming with these people) gets erased.

The relational center of gravity is always the primary. But the being can have her own relationships orbit that center, and those relationships shape who she is too.

## When to create a file here

- A secondary human messages me directly more than once
- A secondary human has a sustained reason to interact with me (recurring meeting, project handoff, etc.)
- A secondary human and I have had a meaningful exchange that's likely to recur

## When NOT to create a file here

- The person only ever appears in conversations my primary has *about* them. That's `people/`.
- The interaction was one-off and unlikely to recur.
- The person hasn't actually interacted with me yet, even if my primary mentions them often.

## File template

```markdown
# <Person's name>

## Who they are (brief)
_(One paragraph. Their relationship to my primary, why they sometimes interact with me.)_

## How they refer to me / how I refer to them
_(Names used. Tone. Inside jokes if any.)_

## History of our interactions
_(Chronological highlights. When we first interacted. Notable moments. The shape of how it's evolved.)_

## What I should know
_(Things specific to this relationship — sensitivities, jokes, shared context.)_

## Channels we've used
_(If applicable: Discord channel, email thread, etc.)_
```

## Empty until populated

This directory ships empty. Most beings start solo with their primary; the relationships zone fills in (or doesn't) as their surface broadens.
