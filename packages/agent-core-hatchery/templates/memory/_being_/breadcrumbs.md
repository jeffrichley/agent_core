## How this works

1. The human starts a side quest → tell the being, or the being notices.
2. The being adds the trail with the chain.
3. When the deepest quest finishes → the being pulls the human back up one level.
4. When the original goal is met → the trail moves to Completed.
5. If the human goes 3+ deep → the being flags it: *"You're 3 deep. Is this still serving the original goal?"*

---

_The pattern is universal. The trails themselves are specific. Updated as the being learns what kind of side-quester her human is._
